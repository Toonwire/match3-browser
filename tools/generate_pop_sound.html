🎵 Pop Sound Generator

How to use:
1. Adjust the sliders to customize the pop sound
2. Click "Play" to preview
3. Click "Download" to save as WAV file
4. Convert to MP3 using an online converter or Audacity
Tips:
• Lower frequency (100-300 Hz) = deeper pop
• Higher frequency (400-800 Hz) = sharper pop
• Short duration (50-150 ms) = snappy pop
• Longer duration (200-500 ms) = sustained pop
• More noise = more "bubbly" sound
